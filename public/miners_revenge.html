The SöKē Board is hard at work getting ready for 2026. This year's races will be on the weekend of July 10th - 12th. Last year's event descriptions are below and will be updated soon. For more information follow us on Facebook.

EVENT DESCRIPTIONS

XC RACES: 1, 2, and 3 Lap Events

Steep climbs, rolling singletrack, fun descents, and riding with bats. Part of the 6 mile lap is through the underground mine. Because of the total darkness a helmet and a GOOD light are required for the XC race. Awards will be given out by age group and gender for 1, 2, and 3 lap races. The age groups are 18U, 18-35, and 36+. Timing is provided by Superior Timing.

BLACK DIAMOND KIDS Adventure Course (Ages 5 - 13)

Welcome to our thrilling youth adventure bike course! This weekend cycling experience is designed for young riders seeking excitement, exploration, and a dash of adrenaline. Whether riding alone, with parents, or friends, participants will embark on a journey through diverse trails filled with challenge and endless fun. As riders set off, they'll encounter challenging terrains, including rocky paths and winding trails adorned with roots, testing their agility and endurance. The course offers appropriate difficulty levels to accommodate both beginners and more advanced riders, ensuring a safe yet exhilarating adventure. Amidst the natural beauty, participants will discover hidden gems, picturesque landscapes, and even encounter local wildlife along the way. So grab your bike, put on your helmet, and get ready for an unforgettable weekend of outdoor thrills. Our youth adventure bike course is the perfect playground for rocks, roots, and most importantly, fun!

ENDURO: Beginner, Sport, and Expert Events

The Enduro is a stage-race format where the goal is to accumulate the lowest combined time from a series of individually timed sections. The transfers are not timed, but you will have cut-off times. Don't forget your light - we will be going underground! Awards will be given to the top three men and top three women overall in the beginner, sport, and expert events, the top three male and female U16 riders, and the top three male and female 40+ riders. Timing provided by Superior Timing.

DOWN HILL

The Downhill consists of two timed runs, best 1 of 2 times wins. Awards will be given for the overall men and women as well as the U16 girls and boys. Timing provided by Superior Timing.

TRIPLE CROWN - Saturday & Sunday

Can you survive a weekend of riding at the mine? Awarded to the fastest male and female in the DH, Long XC, and Expert Enduro events. You must race in all three events to qualify!
